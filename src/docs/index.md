# A-Team's user docs

A-Team's user docs are for people who run agent teams with it. They live in two places, each
where its readers already look:

- **[README](../../README.md)**, on GitHub: what A-Team is, installing it, starting your first
  team by hand, and the command line.
- **[The guide](../../docs/guide/index.md)**, in `docs/guide`: using the app. The app opens these
  same pages under **Help → Guide**, so whoever is in the app reads them there, and anyone on
  GitHub reads them as Markdown.

## [README](../../README.md)

For someone who hasn't installed A-Team yet: what it does, installing it with Homebrew, starting a
team and giving it its own GitHub App, how runs are started and stopped, releasing, and trying out a
pull request.

## [How a team works](../../docs/guide/how-a-team-works.md)

For a new stakeholder: who does what, the stages work moves through on the board, where you decide,
and how Ideas and their priorities steer the Lead.

## [Work](../../docs/guide/work.md)

For the stakeholder at work: everything waiting on you across every team, and answering from it.

## [Overseer](../../docs/guide/overseer.md)

For the stakeholder keeping an eye on things: every team's board on one screen, and the cards that
have waited too long.

## [Dashboard](../../docs/guide/dashboard.md)

For anyone watching or stepping into a run: each agent's pane and log, pausing a role, and
interrupting a run.

## [Teams](../../docs/guide/teams.md)

For setting up and looking after a team: starting one from the app, its settings and roles, pausing
or removing it, and what Settings says when it can't run.
